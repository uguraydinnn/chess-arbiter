Arbiter

Every move is checked against the FIDE Laws of Chess.

  • Three arbiter modes: Off (clock only), Rules check and Tournament. Tournament applies FIDE penalties: an illegal move or an incorrect draw claim gives the opponent extra time
  • Illegal moves are explained in plain language, and the squares involved are marked in red: castling through check, a pinned piece, a piece in the way
  • An arbiter report with every game: rulings, draw offers, clock corrections, each with the time of day and both clocks. Tap a line to see the position at that moment. The last ruling can be undone
More (5)
  • Illegal actions are recorded too: pressing the clock without moving, moving with two hands, an unfinished promotion
  • Threefold repetition and the 50-move rule are tracked; you can claim with your next move
  • Stalemate, insufficient material, fivefold repetition and the 75-move rule end the game automatically
  • On a flag fall, it checks whether the opponent can still mate
  • A live strip under the board shows how often the position has repeated, the 50-move count, illegal moves on each side and whether each side can still mate

Clock

Tap your half of the screen, like a tournament clock.

  • Fischer increment, Bronstein and simple delay, multi-stage classical controls
  • Undo a wrong press. The arbiter can correct the clock, and every correction goes into the report
  • The screen stays on during a game. Turn the phone around on the table and each clock stays with its player
More (3)
  • A different time for Black (handicap) and custom starting positions
  • Hold to pause. A sound and vibration in the last 10 seconds, and a click on every press, both optional
  • An unfinished game resumes if the app is closed

Scoresheet

Your hands stay on the board.

  • Enter moves by dragging or tapping on the board, or by voice: “knight f3”, “short castle”
  • Notation in English (N B R Q K) or Turkish (A F K V Ş) letters
  • Games stay on your phone. Share a game or its arbiter report; export all games as PGN, or import PGN
More (6)
  • Board view for the players facing each other, or with White or Black at the bottom for an arbiter at the side
  • Draw offers, resignation and move-by-move replay. Play on from any moment of a saved game
  • The opening is recognised and saved with its ECO code. Optionally see its name during training games (never in Tournament mode)
  • In a fast game, press the clock and enter the moves later; the app keeps count of what is pending. Set up any position or paste a FEN
  • Player names and event, site and round go into the PGN header
  • The PGN keeps the time left after every move and the arbiter’s rulings as comments, so Lichess or ChessBase can show them

Learn

Learn the rules, and chess, while you play.

  • 16 short lessons with interactive boards, from how the pieces move to the arbiter’s rules
  • 20 classic opening traps: play through the moves, decide what you would play, then see the punishment and the right defence
  • 60 teaching puzzles: mates, forks, pins, skewers, discovered attacks, promotion. Hints are there, and solved puzzles are remembered
More (4)
  • 29 key openings and defences; step through each main line on the board and start a game from any move. A puzzle position can be played out as a game too
  • 16 famous moves from chess history: find the move, then read why it mattered
  • A searchable glossary of 116 terms, from fork and pin to Lucena and Philidor, many with example positions
  • Links to the official FIDE Laws of Chess (2023) and the Turkish Chess Federation’s 2018 Turkish translation

In rated events

In FIDE-rated games, players may not have a phone or other electronic device in the playing area unless the arbiter allows it (FIDE Laws, Article 11.3). Chess Arbiter is for casual, club, school and training games.

No account. No ads. No data collection.

Your games stay on your device. Voice moves are turned into text on the device; if your device doesn't recognise the language, Apple's speech recognition service does it. The app never records or sends your voice.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Everyone who installs it before Pro arrives in 2027 keeps every feature free, including Pro, permanently.

Can I use it in a rated tournament?

No. In FIDE-rated games, players may not have a phone or other electronic device in the playing area unless the arbiter allows it (FIDE Laws, Article 11.3). Chess Arbiter is for casual, club, school and training games.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. The only exception: if your device can't recognise speech on its own, voice entry uses Apple's speech recognition, which needs a connection.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone and iPad. The app is in English and Turkish, and voice entry understands the language the app is set to.

Is it affiliated with FIDE?

No. It follows the FIDE Laws of Chess but is not affiliated with or endorsed by FIDE.
