Features

Rules arbiter

Illegal moves explained in plain language: castling through check, pinned pieces, en passant timing. Tournament mode applies FIDE penalties.

Voice moves

Say “knight f3” or “short castle”. Hands stay on the board; the app keeps score.

Report and PGN

Every game is saved with an arbiter report: rulings, draw offers, clock corrections. Export all games as PGN.

Every time control

Fischer increment, Bronstein and simple delay, multi-stage classical controls, handicap times.

Learn the rules while you play

Short lessons with interactive boards, from how the pieces move to the arbiter's rules.

No account. No ads. No data collection.

Your games stay on your device. Voice moves are turned into text on the device; if your device doesn't recognise the language, Apple's speech recognition service does it. The app stores and sends nothing.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Everyone who installs it before Pro arrives in 2027 keeps every feature free, including Pro, permanently.

Can I use it in a rated tournament?

No. In FIDE-rated games, players may not have a phone or other electronic device in the playing area unless the arbiter allows it (FIDE Laws, Article 11.3). Chess Arbiter is for casual, club, school and training games.

Does it need the internet?

No. It works fully offline.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone and iPad. English and Turkish.

Is it affiliated with FIDE?

No. It follows the FIDE Laws of Chess but is not affiliated with or endorsed by FIDE.
